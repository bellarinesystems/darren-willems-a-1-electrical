What We Do

Electrical Services Across the Ballarat Region

One licensed team, every electrical job — from a single powerpoint to a full industrial fit-out.

Domestic Electrical

From new power points and lighting upgrades to full home rewires and switchboard replacements, we handle every electrical need around the house. All work is carried out to Australian Standards and signed off with a Certificate of Electrical Safety.

Commercial Fit-Outs

We wire shops, offices and hospitality venues across Ballarat with minimal disruption to your trade. Whether it's a new fit-out or upgrading an ageing system, we work to your timeline and budget.

Industrial Electrical

Three-phase installations, machinery wiring, factory maintenance and fault-finding for industrial sites around the region. We understand downtime costs money, so we get in, sort it, and get you running again.

Lighting Design & Installation

LED upgrades, downlights, outdoor and security lighting designed to suit your space and slash your power bill. We'll talk through options that genuinely work for how you use the room.

Safety Switches & Switchboards

Older Ballarat homes often have switchboards that simply aren't up to today's loads. We upgrade boards, install RCDs and safety switches, and make sure your family or staff are properly protected.

Need Something Else?

If you've got an electrical job that doesn't quite fit one of the categories above, give Darren a bell anyway. Chances are we've handled it before — and if not, we'll point you the right way.
